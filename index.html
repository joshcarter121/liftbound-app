Common questions

How do I play? Drag an elevator car to a floor to send it there. Each rider wears the colour of the floor they want. Plan your stops, and good routing stacks up multipliers.

Do I need an account? No. Game Center is optional and only used for leaderboards and achievements.

Are there ads or in-app purchases? No. You pay once and get the whole game.

Does my progress sync? If iCloud is on for your device, your progress follows you between your iPhone and iPad through your own iCloud account.
