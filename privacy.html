Privacy Policy

Liftbound · Effective October 27, 2026

The short version

Liftbound does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.

Your game progress

Your buildings, scores, keepsakes and settings are saved on your device. If iCloud is turned on, they also sync between your own devices through your iCloud account. That data goes to Apple, not to me, and I can't see it.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you sign in, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. Apple handles that data under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Liftbound is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
